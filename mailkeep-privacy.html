Mailkeep Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-02

Mailkeep is an email client for Gmail, published by PNSJY. It runs entirely on your device. There is no Mailkeep server: your email never passes through any system we operate.

Data Mailkeep accesses

When you sign in with Google, Mailkeep asks for the Gmail scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify. It uses this to read, search, label, move, archive, trash, send and draft email on your behalf, which are the features of the app. Your Google account email address and display name are used to show which account is signed in.

How the data is used and stored

  • Email data is fetched directly from Google to your device and used only to provide the features you ask for.
  • A local cache of message metadata (subject, sender, snippet, labels) is kept in an on-device database. Message bodies are not stored. Removing an account deletes its cache; uninstalling the app deletes everything.
  • We do not collect, transmit, sell, share or store your Google user data on any server.
  • No humans read your email. Nobody at PNSJY has access to it.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Mailkeep's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the user-facing email features of the app, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Optional diagnostics

Settings contains an opt-in, off-by-default switch to send anonymous crash and error reports. When enabled, reports contain only app version, OS and coarse device model, and a sanitized error summary. They never contain email content, addresses, file names or credentials. A local diagnostics log is also kept on the device and is shared only if you copy and send it yourself.

Revoking access and deleting data

Remove an account in Mailkeep to delete its local data, and revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Since we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing to request from us.
