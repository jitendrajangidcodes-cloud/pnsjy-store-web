Privacy Policy — Reminder

Application ID: com.pnsjy.reminder_app • Effective Date: November 15, 2026 • Developer: PNSJY / Jitendra K Jangid

Reminder is a 100% offline-first alarm and reminder application for Android phones, foldables, and tablets. It requires no user account, no sign-in, and no cloud subscription.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All data you create in Reminder is stored exclusively inside your device's private application database (SQLite):

  • Reminder titles, notes, schedules, recurrence rules, and priority levels
  • Categories, custom colors, and icons
  • Optional photo attachments and web links added to a reminder
  • Optional map coordinates and radius for location-based reminders
  • Completion history and app preferences

None of your reminders, notes, categories, photos, or location coordinates are uploaded to any server or shared with third parties.

2. Permissions & How They Are Used

  • Exact Alarms & Full-Screen Intent (USE_EXACT_ALARM, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT): Required for the core functionality of waking your device and ringing your scheduled alarms at the exact minute you choose, including over the lock screen.
  • Location & Background Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION): Requested only if you create a location-based reminder (triggering an alarm when you arrive at or leave a chosen place). Location data is evaluated entirely on your device to detect geofence entry/exit even when the app is closed or not in use, and is never transmitted off your device.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Requested only when you open Settings → Receive via QR scan to scan a reminder transfer QR code from another phone or tablet. Camera frames are processed in real time on your device and are never saved or transmitted.
  • Calendar (READ_CALENDAR, WRITE_CALENDAR — Optional): Requested only if you enable the optional local calendar mirror in Settings.

3. Data Portability (QR Transfer & File Backup)

You can transfer or back up your reminders at any time using:

  • QR Device Transfer: Encodes your reminders into an offline GZip-compressed QR code displayed on your screen for another device to scan directly.
  • File Export & Import: Exports a .json full backup or .csv spreadsheet to a folder you choose via the Android system file picker.

4. Optional Technical Diagnostics

Reminder includes an optional, opt-in toggle in Settings (Send anonymous crash & delivery diagnostics, disabled unless enabled by you) that sends sanitized technical error codes to help diagnose alarm delivery issues. It never includes reminder titles, notes, photos, or location coordinates.

5. Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device, deleting a reminder in the app, clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstalling Reminder permanently deletes all your data immediately.
